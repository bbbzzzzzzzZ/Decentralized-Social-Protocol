## English

### 🌟 Project Mission: Humanity Before Profession
Modern society locks individuals into rigid, lifetime "occupations" where organizations own a person’s total availability. The **Decentralized Social Protocol (DSP)** is a thought experiment and an open-source framework designed to decouple human identity from job titles, replacing traditional employment with a dynamic **Skill Pool** and a **Lifetime Responsibility Ledger**.

### ⚙️ Core Operational Framework
The system operates on three simple steps to ensure individual freedom while maintaining social infrastructure:

1.  **Decouple Capabilities from Lifetime Identities:** Individuals hold dynamic portfolios in a **Skill Pool** (e.g., Surgery License A, Coding Capability B). You are a human holding verified skills—not a title owned by an institution.
2.  **Fulfill Public Responsibility Tasks:** Essential societal needs are broken into discrete tasks. You fulfill your bounded civic duty by taking tasks from the **Task Pool** rather than selling 40 years of your life.
3.  **Reclaim Absolute Time Sovereignty:** Once your obligation is cleared, 100% of remaining time is yours. This "Time Leverage" allows high-intensity contribution (e.g., 8 hours of emergency rescue) to clear 16+ hours on the ledger.

## 中文版

### 🌟 项目使命：人在职先 (Humanity Before Profession)
现代社会将个人死死绑定在单一的“职业身份”上，机构不仅购买劳动成果，更占有了个人的终身时间。**去中心化社会协议 (DSP)** 是一个开源框架，旨在通过**“能力池”**与**“终身责任账本”**将人格价值与职业解绑，实现真正的时间主权。

### ⚙️ 核心操作逻辑
本协议通过以下三个步骤，在保障社会运转的同时赢回个人自由：

1.  **技能与职业/身份彻底解绑：** 个人不再被赋予永久标签。你只是在“技能库”里拥有被验证的动态能力（如：外科手术 A 级、编程 B 级），你是拥有技能的完整的人，而非机构的附属品。
2.  **以“公共责任任务”履行义务：** 社会必要劳动被拆解为具体的任务。你通过从“任务池”中自主选单来结清社会责任，而非把几十年的人生卖给特定的科层组织。
3.  **赢回绝对时间主权：** 结清责任后，剩下的时间完全归个人所有。通过“时间杠杆”机制，高浓度的生命付出（如 8 小时紧急救援）可折算为 16 小时甚至更多的账本额度。
