## 💡 Architecture Overview

The Decentralized Social Protocol operates on three fundamental layers:

1. **Constitutional Layer (`01`–`03`, `governance/`):** Establishes human time sovereignty, existential equality, and strict boundaries restricting collective authority.
2. **Algorithmic Coordination Layer (`04`–`07`):** Replaces centralized planning and capital-driven labor with open data ledgers, non-fiat responsibility credits ($E_q$), and dynamic time leverage.
3. **Applied Layer (`examples/`):** Provides stress-tested, real-world domain scenarios verifying protocol resilience under non-coercive constraints.
