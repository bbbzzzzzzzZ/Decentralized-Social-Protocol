# 03_labor_economy.md — Labor, Economy, and Time Sovereignty

## 1. Redefining Labor and De-Jobbing Society

### 1.1 The Critique of Modern Occupational Lock-In
Traditional capitalism locks individuals into lifelong, rigid "jobs" or "careers." An employer buys not merely a specific output or service, but an individual's total ongoing availability and time sovereignty. This creates systemic coercion: individuals must continuously sell their time to avoid existential starvation.

### 1.2 "De-Jobbing" into Capability Registers
In this protocol, society replaces the concept of fixed jobs with a **Modular Capability Registry**:
* **Individual Time Ownership**: Having a registered skill (e.g., surgical qualification, electrical maintenance) does not grant the system or any entity an automatic claim over an individual's available time.
* **Granular Task Counter-Matching**: Labor is unbundled into discrete, time-bound tasks or service windows based on dynamic societal needs, rather than continuous employment contracts.

---

## 2. Axiom of Time Sovereignty

**Time Sovereignty** is the foundational rule governing all labor interactions:

1. **Default Ownership**: An individual's time belongs unconditionally to themselves. Saving time through high productivity returns that time to the individual, rather than triggering additional assigned work.
2. **Active Confirmation over Passive Conscription**: Society or public systems may publish real-time labor shortfalls, but fulfilling them requires explicit, voluntary confirmation by the individual.
3. **Anti-Invasive Protection**: Systemic tracking of capabilities must not degrade into a digital conscription network. Individuals maintain an absolute right to decline non-crisis task requests without algorithmic penalties, reputation downgrades, or loss of baseline survival resources.

## 3. The Dual-Tier Economy: Basic Rights vs. Experience Rights

To eliminate forced exploitation while maintaining societal vitality, the economy operates on a strict **Dual-Tier Model**:

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+


|                       EXPERIENCE & DISCOVERY RIGHTS                   |


|  • Advanced research priority & computing allocation                 |


|  • High-tier material experience & special facility usage             |


|  • Earned via Non-Transferable Public Responsibility Points           |


+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+


========================= UNPASSABLE ISOLATION WALL =========================


+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+


|                      UNIVERSAL SURVIVAL BASELINE                      |


|  • Free, unconditional access to basic food, housing, energy, care    |


|  • Natural resources managed as shared inheritance                    |


|  • Decoupled from labor contribution or market exchange value          |


+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+

### 3.1 Tier 1: Universal Survival Baseline
* **Unconditional Access**: Every individual receives a guaranteed baseline of physical survival (nutrition, housing, basic medical care, clean energy, and education) as a birthright.
* **De-alienation**: Survival is no longer a bargaining chip. No person can be coerced into demeaning or dangerous labor under the threat of poverty or hunger.

### 3.2 Tier 2: Experience Rights & Resource Priorities
* **High-Tier Allocation**: Premium experiences (e.g., priority access to scarce research equipment, high-performance computing clusters, premium event access, specialized travel rights) are governed by contribution rather than capital ownership.
* **Non-Market Counter-Matching**: Access is unlocked through voluntary fulfillment of public responsibility duties rather than financial accumulation.

---

## 4. Anti-Capital Counter-Matching: Non-Transferable Responsibility Points

To prevent the re-emergence of financial oligarchy, class stratification, and speculative exploitation, the economy uses a **Non-Monetary Valuation Framework**:

1. **Non-Transferable & Non-Inheritable**: Public contribution credits (Responsibility Points) are strictly tied to individual identity. They cannot be traded, gifted, inherited, or converted into private ownership of means of production.
2. **Usage Rights vs. Private Property**: Points do not purchase permanent ownership over assets; they grant **Usage & Experience Sovereignty** for designated windows.
3. **Decay and Renewal**: Contribution metrics continuously decay over time to encourage active engagement and prevent permanent aristocratic status.

---

## 5. Systemic Safeguards against Exploitation

To ensure that capability tracking does not devolve into a dystopian labor-extraction engine, the protocol enforces four hard redlines:

* **No Multi-Skill Penalty**: Possessing multiple certified capabilities must never result in disproportionate task dispatching or system pressure.
* **No Algorithmic Favoritism**: Dispatch algorithms are forbidden from prioritizing "highly compliant" or "always available" individuals at the expense of their well-being.
* **Occupational Health Index**: Tasks are continuously evaluated for mental stress and physical strain. High-burnout roles automatically trigger mandatory rest periods and dynamic leverage adjustments.
* **Absolute Right of Exit**: Any participant can pause or revoke their public service availability at any time with zero systemic reprisal.
