
# 04_public_service.md: Public Responsibility & Service System

---

## Introduction: Why Do We Need a Public Responsibility System?

In the discussions of the previous chapters, we established two fundamental inferences:

1. **The existential value of every human being is completely equal**, without class stratification based on occupation, ability, or social contribution.
2. **An individual's life time naturally belongs to themselves (Time Sovereignty)**; society has no right to default any individual's time into available institutional capacity.

However, the operation of a realistic society inevitably relies on the continuity of basic maintenance and public services—including medical treatment, fire rescue, environmental sanitation, energy inspection, infrastructure maintenance, and basic education.

The traditional societal solution is to bind these public maintenance tasks to fixed "lifelong occupations," forcing a subset of individuals to bear long-term high risks, high intensity, and high physical/mental wear and tear. The core innovation of the **Public Responsibility System** strikes straight at the essence: **"The fact that society needs medical and fire-fighting services does not mean that society needs to permanently possess a fixed group of doctors and firefighters."**

By decoupling "lifelong positions" into a **"Skill Pool"** and **"Dynamic Task Quotas,"** the Public Responsibility System achieves a perfect decoupling and balance between **the continuity of public services** and **personal time sovereignty**.

---

## I. Core Axioms and Design Principles

### 1.1 Demand Corresponds to Rights, Rights Correspond to Responsibilities

* **Unconditional Guarantee of Survival Baseline**: The social community unconditionally provides all members with dignified basic survival security (food, housing, basic medical care, and education), eliminating the dead-end of being forced into labor due to survival panic.
* **Reciprocal Public Responsibility Quota**: As a reciprocal prerequisite for enjoying the community's baseline security and public resources, adult citizens with working capacity bear a limited, quantifiable "public responsibility time quota" within their life cycle.

### 1.2 Humans Are Not Occupational Identities, but Skill Collections

* The system no longer records in archives that "Person X is a doctor/electrician/teacher," but rather records "which specific skills Person X holds and has verified (e.g., Surgery-Level, Emergency Rescue-Level, Basic Teaching-Level)".
* Individuals can simultaneously hold multiple skill certifications and undertake public tasks in different fields during different periods according to personal preference.

### 1.3 Unity of Responsibility and Personal Development

* Public responsibility is no longer organized as a consumable that competes with personal development; instead, through a dynamic adjustment mechanism, fulfilling public responsibility becomes an active choice to exchange for higher-tier leisure time and research resources.

## II. Four-Tier Hierarchy of Public Tasks and the Dynamic Time Leverage Model

Not all public tasks consume the same amount of energy and cost. The system must acknowledge learning costs, risks, and physical/mental wear, adjusting the reward equivalence of different tasks through a **Dynamic Time Leverage** model rather than using currency.

| Task Tier | Typical Representatives | Entry Threshold / Qualification Verification | Physical & Mental Burden & Risk | Responsibility Credit Leverage Multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1: High Threshold / High Risk** | Surgeons, high-voltage power grid maintenance, disaster rescue | Extremely high (long-term professional training, simulator re-training, and peer certification) | Extremely high (involves safety of life and major system risks) | **3.0x - 5.0x** (High leverage reward) |
| **Tier 2: High Intensity / High Monotony** | Deep-well mining, waste sorting/treatment, underground pipeline maintenance | Lower or moderate (focuses on operational standards and safety protection) | Extremely high (physical exhaustion, harsh environment, high repetition) | **2.0x - 3.0x** (High leverage compensation) |
| **Tier 3: Routine Maintenance / Care** | Community nurses, routine public facility inspection, basic education | Moderate (standardized skills and psychological quality assessment) | Moderate (emotional labor or regular physical labor) | **1.0x - 1.5x** (Standard reward) |
| **Tier 4: General / Basic Rotation** | Community environmental order guidance, basic public sorting assistance | Extremely low (no technical requirements, covered by universal compulsory education) | Low (mild physical and daily assistance) | **0.8x - 1.0x** (Baseline reward) |

### Design Logic and Breakthroughs:

1. **Breaking the Lifelong Trap at the Bottom**: Undertaking higher-risk, harder, or longer-training public tasks yields exceptionally high responsibility credit equivalents within a very short actual working time, enabling the exchange of massive amounts of completely free time for self-exploration and research.
2. **Tier 4 Public Task Rotation**: Non-technical Tier 4 tasks prioritize an "all-staff baseline rotation mechanism," covering graduation transition periods for students and basic responsibility fulfillment needs for those without specific skill registrations.

## III. Dynamic Contracts and Decentralized Dispatch Mechanisms

### 3.1 Decentralized Skill Pool and Grid-based Order-grabbing

* **Transparent Gap Publication**: The data hub only publishes real-time public demand gaps (e.g., doctor-patient ratio gaps in specific regions, facility repair gaps) without direct administrative scheduling.
* **Autonomous Response**: Individuals holding corresponding qualification certifications autonomously decide whether to accept orders to fulfill responsibilities based on the time leverage multiplier published by the system and their own schedules. If a task gap widens, the system automatically increases its time leverage multiplier until sufficient social capability response is attracted.

### 3.2 Responsibility Time Quota and Free Rhythms

* **Lifecycle / Periodic Settlement**: Public responsibility sets a total quota over an annual or longer cycle (e.g., 400 hours of basic public responsibility per year) rather than a fixed 40-hour weekly work system.
* **High Freedom of Time Selection**: Individuals can choose to sprint and complete their full year's public responsibility quota at the beginning of the year, subsequently enjoying a full half-year of undisturbed free writing or rest periods; or they can choose to undertake public responsibility one fixed day per week.

### 3.3 Wartime and Emergency Overdrive Protocol

To cope with major natural disasters, public health emergencies, or system-level major accidents, the system establishes a strict emergency mobilization mechanism:

1. **Strict Objective Activation Conditions**: Triggered by physical indicators (e.g., continuous disaster data reaching critical thresholds); algorithms only provide warnings, and decisions are publicly made by accountable subjects.
2. **Limited Power Coverage**: Under wartime mode, skill holders who have previously signed emergency contracts are temporarily invoked, and their time sovereignty is subject to a limited temporary concession.
3. **Double Compensation and Mandatory Recovery**: Fulfilling duties in wartime grants double/high-tier responsibility credits and mandatory rest periods. Emergency states set clear time limits and geographical scopes, automatically expiring upon expiration and subject to post-event auditing.

---

## IV. Responsibility Credit Equivalences and Anti-Class Restoration Mechanisms

### 4.1 Absolute Attributes of Responsibility Credits

* **Non-monetizable and Non-financialized**: Responsibility credits can absolutely not be transferred, borrowed, inherited, or converted into private property of means of production, fundamentally eliminating capital accumulation and financial exploitation.
* **Attenuation and Zeroing Mechanism**: Credits are only valid within an individual's life cycle; unused excess credits over time automatically and smoothly attenuate, preventing cross-generational accumulation.

### 4.2 Experience Rights and Research Resource Priority

Equivalent rewards obtained through over-fulfilling public responsibility or undertaking ultra-high-difficulty tasks are primarily reflected in:

1. **High-Tier Quality Material Experience Rights**: Redeeming use and experience rights for high-quality living spaces and high-tier daily living supplies, rather than buying out property rights.
2. **Top-Tier Research and Production Resource Priority**: Providing genuine creators and researchers with top-tier laboratory use rights, large computing power quotas, and precision experimental equipment scheduling rights.
3. **Priority Proposal Rights on Public Issues**: Gaining priority proposal and debate channels in major public deliberations of the community.

---

## V. Edge Cases and System Defense Design

### 5.1 Refusal to Fulfill Duties and Free-Riding

* **Minor Absence (e.g., forgotten, time management errors)**: Arrange make-up tasks and add a small tiered responsibility duration as a penalty.
* **Malicious Evasion / Score Fraud**: Restrict non-basic public welfare benefits (e.g., revoking high-tier experience zone access rights, suspending qualifications for high-trust positions), lowering qualifications for high-trust posts, **without ever depriving them of basic survival baseline security**.

### 5.2 "Skill Decay" of High-Threshold Skills

* For skills highly reliant on clinical/practical touch such as high-altitude repairs or surgical operations, individuals freed up for creative pursuits must regularly participate in simulator re-certifications and random peer spot checks.
* Those failing simulator exams will have their "high-leverage order-taking qualifications" suspended, requiring short-cycle clinical assistance completion before recovery.

### 5.3 Anti-Gatekeeping

* **Decentralized Cross-Certification**: Qualification certifications and peer evaluations adopt randomized, anonymous, cross-regional reviews.
* **Fulfilling Process Evidence Preservation**: The entire process of order acceptance and evaluation for public tasks is preserved on-chain/evidenced, eliminating phenomena where senior peers monopolize high-credit positions or obstruct newcomers from entering the pool through cliquish behavior.

---

## Summary

The Public Responsibility System ensures that society no longer relies on exploiting the lifelong time of specific groups to maintain operations. It transforms public maintenance into an efficient, spontaneous synergy among all citizens based on capabilities, contracts, and free will, completely eliminating class exploitation while providing a solid, unshakeable material and social foundation for human free exploration.
