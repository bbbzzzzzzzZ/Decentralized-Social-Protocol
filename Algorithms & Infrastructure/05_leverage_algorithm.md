### 📄 05_leverage_algorithm.md: Dynamic Time Leverage & Decentralized Scheduling Algorithm

**Document ID:** 05_leverage_algorithm.md  
**Core Objective:** To completely replace traditional bureaucratic administrative scheduling and capitalist wage labor with decentralized data feedback and dynamic pricing mechanisms, ensuring the continuous operation of society while maximizing individual time sovereignty.

---

#### I. Foundational Axioms of Algorithm Design
1.  **Decentralization:** The system contains no administrative center with subjective task-assignment powers. The algorithm is solely responsible for "computation and public disclosure," possessing no right of mandatory requisition over any individual.
2.  **Non-Fiat Credit:** The output regulated by the algorithm consists of **"Responsibility Credits / Dynamic Time Leverage,"** which can only be redeemed for experiential access to high-tier public resources, research computing power, and leisure time. They can never be converted into private ownership of the means of production.
3.  **Sovereignty Supremacy:** Every system prompt or notification is merely an "invitation/request." The registration of a skill does not equate to permanent standby availability, and individuals retain an absolute, penalty-free **Right of Refusal**.

#### II. Module 1: Real-Time Gap Calculation Model
The system determines societal needs (e.g., the number of required doctors or technicians) by generating an **"Absolute Gap" (\\(G_i\\))** based on dynamic indicators and safety thresholds.

**2.1 Gap Calculation Formula**  
For any public maintenance domain \\(i\\) at time \\(t\\), the gap \\(G_i(t)\\) is defined as:  
\\[G_i(t) = \max\left(0, \ D_i(t) - S_i(t)\right)\\]
*   **\\(D_i(t)\\) (Rigid Demand Threshold):** A dynamic standard determined by urban population data, historical disaster probabilities, WHO safety baselines, and physical wear rates.
*   **\\(S_i(t)\\) (Effective Supply):** The total service capacity currently registered in the skill pool and in a "responsive status".

#### III. Module 2: Dynamic Time Leverage Multiplier Model
To solve the dilemma where "dirty, exhausting, or high-threshold jobs" find no takers, the system introduces the **Time Leverage Multiplier (\\(L_i\\))**.

**3.1 Core Variables Determining Leverage**  
*   **Training & Qualification Barrier Cost (\\(C_{train}\\)):** Time required for specialization and peer-review certification difficulty.
*   **Physical/Psychological Burden & Risk Index (\\(R_{risk}\\)):** Degree of involvement regarding safety, extreme environments, or emotional labor.
*   **Real-Time Supply-Demand Ratio (\\(R_{demand}\\)):** The ratio between current gap \\(G_i(t)\\) and available responders.

**3.2 Task Stratification and Leverage Matrix**
| Task Tier | Typical Representatives | Risk & Burden | Leverage Multiplier (\\(L_i\\)) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: High Risk** | Surgery, Disaster rescue | Extremely High | **3.0x - 5.0x** |
| **Tier 2: High Intensity** | Deep mining, Waste processing | High | **2.0x - 3.0x** |
| **Tier 3: Routine Maint.** | Community nursing, Inspections | Medium | **1.0x - 1.5x** |
| **Tier 4: General** | Order guidance, Sorting assist | Low | **0.8x - 1.0x** |

#### IV. Module 3: Decentralized Matching & Surge Pricing
*   **Uber-Style Autonomous Matching:** Users view real-time gaps and leverage multipliers via their terminals. Qualified individuals independently accept tasks based on their own schedules.
*   **Time-Decay Auto-Surge:** If a task remains unfulfilled, the algorithm triggers tiered surge pricing, automatically raising the leverage multiplier every few minutes until sufficient response is attracted.
*   **Physical Fallback:** In extreme circumstances (disasters or system failure), the system initiates a **Circuit Breaker** to trigger local community rotation squads.

#### V. Summary: Balancing Efficiency and Freedom
This algorithm ensures that essential modern infrastructure continues to operate without collapse while allowing individuals to maintain absolute time sovereignty. It achieves efficiency without capital accumulation or bureaucratic coercion.
