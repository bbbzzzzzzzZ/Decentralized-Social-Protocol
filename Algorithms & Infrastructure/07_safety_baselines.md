### 📄 07_safety_baselines.md: System Safety Baselines and Anti-Corruption Mechanisms

**Document ID:** 07_safety_baselines.md  
**Core Objective:** To establish structural safeguards, cryptographic boundaries, and algorithmic circuit breakers that ensure the protocol remains resilient against human fatigue, institutional corruption, and technological creep.

---

#### I. Risk Category 1: Digital Conscription & The "Good Worker Trap"
**The Risk:** In a system where tasks are dynamically matched, highly skilled or cooperative individuals face **Algorithmic Over-Utilization**. The system naturally routes a disproportionate volume of tasks toward them, effectively converting voluntary responsibility into involuntary, continuous forced labor.

**Structural Defenses:**
*   **Hard Volume Ceilings:** Strict protocol-level limits on the total public responsibility hours any individual can undertake within a rolling timeframe. Once reached, the node is automatically locked out of task pools.
*   **Enforced Recovery Intervals:** Following high-intensity tasks (e.g., emergency medical rescue), the system mandates a cooling-off period during which capability registration is temporarily suspended.
*   **Absolute Right of Refusal:** Matching is strictly an *invitation*, never a summons. Declining carries zero penalty to basic survival entitlements.

#### II. Risk Category 2: Credit Monetization & Stratification
**The Risk:** If responsibility credits (\\(E_q\\)) become transferable or accumulatable across generations, they will inevitably crystallize into a new form of fiat currency and private capital, resurrecting class stratification.

**Structural Defenses:**
*   **Strictly Non-Transferable:** Credits cannot be gifted, traded, or lent. Any attempted commercialization instantly revokes the balance.
*   **Non-Convertibility:** Credits only unlock experiential access or advanced computation; they can **never** be converted into private ownership of land, factories, or core infrastructure.
*   **Time-Decay Function:** Accumulated credits are subject to continuous decay. Credits represent recent active contribution, preventing individuals from resting on historical laurels indefinitely.

#### III. Risk Category 3: Privacy Infiltration & Surveillance Creep
**The Risk:** Optimization algorithms may creep into private lives, tracking personal movements or social preferences under the pretext of "maximizing societal efficiency".

**Structural Defenses:**
*   **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP):** The system verifies that a condition is met (e.g., a valid surgical certification) without accessing underlying identity or history.
*   **Data Minimization Principle:** The system is architecturally prohibited from storing personal activity logs outside of explicit, consented public contribution records.

#### IV. Risk Category 4: Professional Monopolies & Peer Gatekeeping
**The Risk:** Practitioners may erect artificial barriers to specialized fields, transforming decentralized professional guilds into self-serving monopolies.

**Structural Defenses:**
*   **Transparent Multi-Path Accreditation:** Decouples accreditation from subjective gatekeeping, relying on open-source simulations and reproducible empirical contributions.
*   **Pluralistic Reviewer Pools:** Randomly sampled and rotated peer evaluation panels to prevent entrenched cliques.

#### V. Risk Category 5: Emergency Overreach & Permanent States of Exception
**The Risk:** Emergency powers, once invoked for a crisis, may become permanent, eroding personal sovereignty under the banner of "societal security".

**Structural Defenses:**
*   **Objective Activation Triggers:** Emergency modes require verified, objective statistical thresholds (e.g., systemic medical capacity breach).
*   **Narrow Functional Scope:** Mobilization can only invoke capabilities relevant to the specific crisis and cannot suspend broader civil liberties.
*   **Mandatory Sunset Clauses:** Every emergency protocol has a hardcoded maximum lifespan. Unless explicitly re-voted by decentralized consensus, the system automatically executes a hard reset to standard peacetime parameters.

---

**Summary:** 
The ultimate metric of the protocol’s success is its ability to sustain civilization while ensuring that **no individual’s freedom, dignity, or time is ever sacrificed to the machinery of the collective**.
