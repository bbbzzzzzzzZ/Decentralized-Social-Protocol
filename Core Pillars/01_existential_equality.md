### 📄 01_existential_equality.md: Existential Equality and the Reconstruction of Meaning

**Core Objective:** To deconstruct the excessive binding of human identity to professional output in modern society and establish the unconditional equality of human existential value as the foundational axiom of the protocol.

---

#### I. Cognitive Science Context: The Brain’s Demand for Meaning
*   **The Brain as a Prediction Engine:** The brain is not merely a thinking organ but a "prediction machine". It constantly seeks models to explain the world, predict the future, and guide action. When these models fail (e.g., when the link between action and survival is broken or forced), individuals experience profound suffering known as the "loss of meaning".
*   **The Crisis of Transparency:** In the modern crisis of meaning, once individuals perceive that "meaning" is a cognitive construct rather than an inherent property of the world, the automatic systems supporting action may stall. This leads to a "legitimacy crisis of action," where the brain requires a self-consistent reason to approve every movement.

#### II. The Core Axiom: Absolute Equality of Existential Value
*   **Unconditional Equality:** The **Existential Value** of a human being is absolute and equal. It is not partitioned by intelligence, capability, profession, wealth, or social contribution. This is the first axiom of the Decentralized Social Protocol.
*   **Rejection of Computable Values:** The protocol rejects any attempt to digitize life or rank individuals based on "worth". Because every life is embedded in a complex causal network with unpredictable future impacts (e.g., a cleaner’s child might become a world-changing scientist), calculating "which life is more valuable" is logically impossible and ethically prohibited.

#### III. The Decoupling Principle: Personality Value vs. Exchange Value
*   **Four Dimensions of Value:** To prevent systemic overreach, the protocol strictly distinguishes between four dimensions:
    1.  **Existential Value:** Equal and non-exchangeable.
    2.  **Behavioral Value:** Accountability for choices.
    3.  **Contribution Value:** The scale of social impact.
    4.  **Exchange Value:** Market pricing for specific skills.
*   **Ending Identity-by-Occupation:** Modern systems conflate "labor exchange value" with "human personality value," forcing individuals to prove they are "useful" to justify their existence. This module establishes the principle of **"Humanity before Profession"**—a profession is a temporary functional role assumed for a specific task, not a permanent identity tag.

#### IV. Philosophical Foundation: Humans as Ends, Not Means
*   **Rejection of Instrumentalization:** Echoing Kantian ethics, human beings must always be treated as **"ends in themselves"** rather than tools for achieving GDP growth, technological progress, or societal efficiency.
*   **Society Serving the Individual:** The underlying logic is flipped from "humans adapting to the system" to "systems adapting to humans". The only justification for society's existence is to serve as an auxiliary tool for individual freedom and growth.

#### V. Systemic Redlines: The Inviolable Sphere
*   **The Constitutional Firewall:** The protocol establishes an **Inviolable Sphere** (Absolute Individual Autonomy). Within this sphere, an individual’s beliefs, lifestyle, cognitive privacy, and time sovereignty are absolute. No collective vote, algorithmic optimization, or public service deficit has the authority to penetrate or revoke these rights.

#### VI. Social Vision: Reconstructing Recognition
*   **Return to Mutual Aid:** Drawing on 300,000 years of egalitarian hunter-gatherer history, the protocol aims to reconstruct human relationships based on unconditional recognition of each other's "existence" rather than social rank.
*   **Eliminating Status Discrimination:** The goal is to eliminate value distortions (such as children despising parents for low social status). Social status should only represent different role allocations, not a hierarchy of life.
