### 📄 02_time_sovereignty.md: Time Sovereignty and Individual Autonomy

**Core Objective:** To establish life-time as an inalienable and irreversible ultimate resource, decoupling survival security from lifelong employment, and ensuring individuals maintain absolute control over their lives after fulfilling finite social responsibilities.

---

#### I. The Core Axiom: Default Ownership of Time
*   **Irreversibility of Life-Time:** Time is the only resource that cannot be recovered through technology or capital. Therefore, the primary legitimacy of any social system must be rooted in respect for individual time.
*   **Individual by Default:** Within this protocol, personal time belongs to the individual by default, not as available capacity for institutions. The mere existence of unutilized time or certified professional skills does not constitute a societal claim over that resource.
*   **From Passive Requisition to Active Consent:** While the system may publish public service needs, an individual’s availability must be actively confirmed and consented to. The system is architecturally prohibited from assuming a "standby" status for any participant.

#### II. The Foundational Floor: Decoupling Survival from Labor
*   **Universal Survival Baseline:** The community provides unconditional access to dignified basic needs—food, housing, healthcare, and connectivity.
*   **Elimination of Survival Panic:** True time sovereignty is only physically possible when an individual is no longer forced to sell their time or dignity under the threat of hunger or homelessness. Survival security transforms labor from "enslavement for existence" into "collaboration based on responsibility and interest".

#### III. Operational Mechanism: Finite Responsibility and Lifetime Banking
*   **Public Responsibility Quotas:** Citizens fulfill a limited, quantifiable quota of "public responsibility hours" over their lifecycle. This replaces the traditional 40-hour-a-week lifelong grind with a flexible social contract that can be fulfilled across different life stages.
*   **Reclaiming Freedom:** Once an individual settles their designated public responsibility tasks, 100% of the remaining time belongs to them. The system has no authority to audit, optimize, or dictate how this "free time" is used—whether for research, art, or pure leisure.
*   **Lifecycle Flexibility:** Individuals may front-load their responsibilities during high-energy periods (e.g., youth) to "buy back" years or even decades of absolute freedom later in life.

#### IV. Structural Defense: Protecting Against "Capability Requisition"
*   **The Right of Refusal:** Possessing a high-demand skill (e.g., surgical expertise) does not imply a duty to be constantly available. The protocol strictly forbids the system from automatically assigning tasks based on skill registration.
*   **The Firewall of Sovereignty:** Time sovereignty serves as the firewall preventing the "Skill Pool" from degenerating into a universal labor conscription system. Task matching only occurs within "availability windows" explicitly opened by the individual.

#### V. The Life Leverage: Density and Equivalent Value
*   **Non-Linear Time Conversion (Time Leverage):** The protocol acknowledges the varying physical and mental tolls of different types of labor. Discrete tasks characterized by high risk, high stress, or extreme urgency (e.g., emergency rescue, polar research) enjoy a high leverage multiplier.
*   **8 Hours Equals 16 Hours:** When an individual invests high-intensity life-time during society's moments of greatest need, their 8-hour contribution may be credited as 16 hours or more on the responsibility ledger.
*   **Trading Density for Length:** This mechanism allows individuals to accumulate credits through "high-intensity burning," effectively extending their free time within the physical dimension of their lifespan.
