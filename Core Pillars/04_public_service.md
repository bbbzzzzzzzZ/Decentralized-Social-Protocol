### 📄 04_public_service.md: Public Responsibility & Service System

**Core Objective:** To ensure the continuous operation of essential societal functions (healthcare, infrastructure, safety) without the use of permanent occupational binding, utilizing a dynamic coordination mechanism based on verified skills and time-leverage incentives.

---

#### Introduction: Why Do We Need a Public Responsibility System?
The Decentralized Social Protocol establishes that human existential value is equal and that life-time naturally belongs to the individual (Time Sovereignty). However, a functioning society requires the continuity of basic services like medicine, fire rescue, and infrastructure maintenance. 

The traditional solution—binding people to lifelong fixed occupations—leads to high burnout and the exploitation of specific groups. This module **decouples "lifelong positions" into a "Skill Pool" and "Dynamic Task Quotas,"** allowing public services to remain continuous while returning time sovereignty to the individual.

---

#### I. Core Axioms and Design Principles
*   **Unconditional Survival Baseline:** The community provides dignified basic needs (food, housing, healthcare, education) unconditionally, eliminating labor forced by survival panic.
*   **Reciprocal Responsibility Quotas:** As a prerequisite for enjoying communal resources, adult citizens bear a limited, quantifiable "public responsibility time quota" within their life cycle.
*   **Capability Over Identity:** The system does not record permanent identities like "doctor" or "electrician." It records verified, modular skills (e.g., Surgery-Level A, Grid Maintenance-Level B).
*   **Unity of Responsibility and Development:** Fulfilling public responsibility is an active choice to exchange specialized labor for higher-tier leisure time and research resources.

---

#### II. Four-Tier Hierarchy of Public Tasks and Dynamic Time Leverage
Tasks are categorized by difficulty and risk, with rewards calculated through **Dynamic Time Leverage** rather than currency.

| Task Tier | Examples | Entry / Qualification | Risk & Burden | Leverage Multiplier (\\(L_i\\)) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: High Risk/Barrier** | Surgeons, disaster rescue | Extremely High (Peer certification/training) | Extremely High (Life safety) | **3.0x - 5.0x** |
| **Tier 2: High Intensity** | Deep mining, waste treatment | Moderate (Safety focus) | High (Physical/Environment) | **2.0x - 3.0x** |
| **Tier 3: Routine Maint.** | Community nurses, inspections | Moderate (Standardized skills) | Moderate (Emotional/Physical) | **1.0x - 1.5x** |
| **Tier 4: General Rotation** | Order guidance, sorting assist | Extremely Low (Universal education) | Low (Daily assistance) | **0.8x - 1.0x** |

**Design Logic:** Higher-risk or harder tasks yield more responsibility credits in less actual time, breaking the "lifelong trap" for intensive laborers by allowing them to quickly "buy back" their free time.

---

#### III. Decentralized Dispatch and Dynamic Contracts
*   **Transparent Gap Publication:** The data hub publishes real-time public demand gaps (e.g., a specific region's doctor-patient ratio) without direct administrative scheduling.
*   **Autonomous Response:** Individuals decide whether to accept tasks based on the published leverage multiplier and their own schedules.
*   **Periodic Settlement:** Responsibility quotas are set over annual or longer cycles (e.g., 400 hours/year), allowing individuals to sprint-complete their duties to earn months of undisturbed freedom.

---

#### IV. Emergency Overdrive Protocol
For natural disasters or public health crises, the system switches to an emergency mode:
1.  **Objective Triggers:** Activated by physical indicators (e.g., systemic capacity breach), not administrative fiat.
2.  **Limited Power:** Skill holders with emergency contracts are invoked, temporarily conceding time sovereignty.
3.  **Compensation:** Fulfilling duties during emergencies grants double/triple credits and mandatory rest periods.

---

#### V. Responsibility Credits and Anti-Class Restoration
To prevent the revival of class stratification, **Responsibility Credits (\\(E_q\\))** have unique attributes:
*   **Non-Monetizable:** Credits cannot be transferred, gifted, bartered, or used as collateral. They can **never** be converted into private ownership of means of production.
*   **Decay Mechanism:** Credits represent recent active contribution and are subject to a time-decay function to prevent permanent forts of privilege.
*   **Experience Rights:** Credits unlock high-tier experiential assets:
    1.  **Material Experience:** High-quality living spaces or special daily supplies.
    2.  **Research Priority:** Top-tier lab access, large computing power, and precision equipment.

---

#### VI. System Defense and Edge Cases
*   **"Good Worker Trap" Prevention:** Protocol-level hard ceilings on total hours and mandatory rest intervals prevent the system from over-utilizing efficient individuals.
*   **Absolute Right of Refusal:** Task matching is an invitation, never a summons. Declining carries zero penalty to basic survival entitlements.
*   **Skill Decay:** High-stakes skill holders (e.g., surgeons) must participate in simulator re-certifications. Failure results in suspended "high-leverage" status until re-verified.
*   **Anti-Gatekeeping:** Peer evaluations are randomized and anonymous to prevent cliquish monopolies in specialized fields.

---

**Summary:** 
The Public Responsibility System replaces retributive coercion with **transparent cryptographic mediation and restorative resource control**. It ensures that no individual's freedom or time is ever sacrificed to the machinery of the collective while maintaining the essential foundations of civilization.
