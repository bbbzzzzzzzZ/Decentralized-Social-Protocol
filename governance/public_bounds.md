### 📄 08_public_bounds.md: Public Bounds and Collective Governance

**Document ID:** governance/public_bounds.md  
**Core Objective:** To establish strict constitutional and algorithmic boundaries for collective decision-making, ensuring that decentralized coordination never encroaches upon individual time sovereignty, existential equality, or lifestyle autonomy while maintaining efficient public infrastructure.

---

#### Introduction: The Paradox of Decentralized Governance
The Decentralized Social Protocol relies on transparent data coordination and dynamic time leverage to sustain civilization without permanent employment or bureaucratic scheduling. However, any collective system—no matter how distributed—faces a fundamental political question: **Where does the authority of the collective end, and where does the absolute sovereignty of the individual begin?**

Historical governance has failed by oscillating between **Centralized Autocracy** (bureaucratic control) and **Majority Tyranny** (peer coercion through 51% voting). To prevent these, this protocol introduces the **Principle of Strict Jurisdictional Bounds**, defining governance solely as a coordination utility rather than a mechanism for social engineering.

#### I. The Two-Tiered Jurisdictional Axiom
The protocol establishes an immutable firewall dividing social reality into two distinct domains: **The Inviolable Sphere** and **The Coordinate Sphere**.

1.  **The Inviolable Sphere (Absolute Individual Autonomy)**  
    No collective vote or algorithmic optimization can breach this domain. It encompasses:
    *   **Time Sovereignty:** An individual's unallocated time and attention belong exclusively to them. The system may issue requests, but it can never compel or penalize non-responsiveness.
    *   **Existential & Lifestyle Autonomy:** Personal values, beliefs, and creative pursuits are immune to collective governance. There is no behavioral policing.
    *   **Cognitive and Data Privacy:** Verified via Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP), the protocol only checks functional compliance without peering into personal motivations or communications.

2.  **The Coordinate Sphere (Limited Collective Management)**  
    Collective governance is strictly confined to:
    *   **Shared Natural Resources:** Managing ecological carrying capacities and physical land use for sustainable access.
    *   **Public Emergency Protocols:** Activating time-bound, strictly audited thresholds for crisis responses.
    *   **Interface Standards:** Maintaining the open-source cryptographic code and transparency ledgers upon which the protocol runs.

#### II. Mechanisms Against Majority Tyranny
The protocol implements structural defenses even within the Coordinate Sphere to protect niche populations:
*   **The Supermajority & Sub-Group Veto:** Alterations to core axioms require near-unanimous consensus across nodes. Any policy disproportionately impacting a minority lifestyle triggers an automatic algorithmic veto for redrafting.
*   **De-financialization of Influence:** Public responsibility credits and experiential quotas (\\(E_q\\)) grant **zero voting power**. Political influence cannot be accumulated, bought, or inherited.
*   **Algorithmic Transparency:** Proposals cannot use emotional rhetoric; they must be submitted as verifiable logic models or simulations, evaluated based on resource impact rather than popularity.

#### III. Non-Permanent Governance and Rotating Stewardship
The protocol rejects the concept of "professional politicians" in favor of:
1.  **Task-Specific Assemblies:** When a coordination problem arises (e.g., energy grid redesign), a temporary, randomized **Stewardship Assembly** is convened from the verified skill pool.
2.  **Immediate Dissolution:** Once the task is validated and deployed, the assembly dissolves. Members return to their personal lives; no permanent office or legislative authority is retained.
3.  **Open Auditability:** Every decision is permanently recorded on the **Transparent Data Center** ledger for public audit, eliminating backroom deals.

---

**Summary:** 
The Public Bounds module proves that complex civilization can coordinate its survival without sacrificing human dignity or time. By walling off the **Inviolable Sphere**, it ensures that society serves the individual, rather than the other way around.
