**Document ID:** `governance/public_bounds.md`

**Core Objective:** Establish strict constitutional and algorithmic boundaries for collective decision-making, ensuring that decentralized coordination never encroaches upon individual time sovereignty, existential equality, or lifestyle autonomy, while maintaining efficient public infrastructure maintenance.

## Introduction: The Paradox of Decentralized Governance

As established in the preceding chapters, the Decentralized Social Protocol relies on transparent data coordination, dynamic time leverage, and non-fiat responsibility credits to sustain civilization without permanent employment or bureaucratic scheduling. However, any collective system—no matter how distributed or mathematically optimized—inevitably faces a fundamental political question: **Where does the authority of the collective end, and where does the absolute sovereignty of the individual begin?**

Historical governance models have perpetually oscillated between two extremes:

1. **Centralized Autocracy:** A permanent ruling class or bureaucratic apparatus dictates social priorities, labor allocation, and moral standards under the guise of "common good."
2. **Majority Tyranny (Direct Democracy Trap):** Unbounded collective voting allows 51% of the population to systematically outvote, marginalize, or impose cultural/lifestyle constraints on the remaining 49%, replacing state coercion with peer coercion.

To prevent these pathologies, the Decentralized Social Protocol introduces the **Principle of Strict Jurisdictional Bounds**. Governance within this framework is not a mechanism for social engineering or moral homogenization; it is strictly a **coordination utility** dedicated to maintaining shared infrastructure and preserving the existential floor.

---

## I. The Two-Tiered Jurisdictional Axiom

The protocol establishes an immutable constitutional firewall dividing all social reality into two distinct domains: **The Inviolable Sphere** and **The Coordinate Sphere**.

### 1. The Inviolable Sphere (Absolute Individual Autonomy)

No collective vote, algorithmic optimization score, or public responsibility deficit can breach the Inviolable Sphere. This domain encompasses:

* **Time Sovereignty:** An individual's unallocated time, attention, and cognitive rhythms belong exclusively to them. The system may issue requests or publish public service deficits, but it can never compel, automatically assign, or penalize individuals for non-responsiveness.
* **Existential & Lifestyle Autonomy:** Personal values, philosophical beliefs, aesthetic choices, interpersonal associations, and creative pursuits are entirely immune to collective governance. There is no "social norm enforcement" or community-driven behavioral policing through protocol layers.
* **Cognitive and Data Privacy:** As defined by Zero-Knowledge proofs (ZKP), the protocol only verifies functional compliance (e.g., medical license validity) without peering into personal motivations, private communications, or lifestyle logs.

### 2. The Coordinate Sphere (Limited Collective Management)

Collective governance is strictly confined to the Coordinate Sphere, which includes:

* **Shared Natural Resources:** Managing ecological carrying capacities, energy grids, and physical land use to ensure sustainable intergenerational access.
* **Public Emergency Protocols:** Establishing transparent, time-bound, and strictly audited thresholds for activating crisis responses (e.g., pandemics, natural disasters) as defined in Chapter 06.
* **Interface Standards & Open Protocols:** Maintaining the core cryptographic code, data center transparency ledgers, and open-source infrastructure upon which decentralized coordination runs.

## II. Mechanisms Against Majority Tyranny

Even within the Coordinate Sphere, unconstrained voting can lead to systemic oppression of minorities or unconventional lifestyles. The protocol implements three structural defenses:

### 1. The Supermajority & Sub-Group Veto

* **Structural Modifications:** Alterations to the core protocol axioms (such as existential equality or the non-fiat nature of credits) require near-unanimous consensus across distributed nodes, preventing temporary political majorities from rewriting foundational rights.
* **Harm Asymmetry Rule:** Any collective policy that disproportionately impacts a minority group's lifestyle or time sovereignty triggers an automatic algorithmic veto, requiring redrafting to ensure zero negative externalities on vulnerable or niche populations.

### 2. De-financialization of Influence

* In traditional systems, wealth translates into political lobbying power. In the Decentralized Social Protocol:
* Public responsibility credits are **non-transferable, non-lendable, and strictly non-convertible into capital**.
* Experiential quotas ($E_q$) grant access to research equipment and high-end living spaces, but **zero voting power or legislative leverage**.
* Political influence cannot be accumulated, bought, or inherited.



### 3. Algorithmic Transparency over Political Rhetoric

* Governance proposals cannot be shaped by charismatic rhetoric, marketing campaigns, or emotional persuasion. All proposals must be submitted as verifiable, open-source logic models or resource-allocation simulations. The system evaluates proposals based on projected resource impact and adherence to safety baselines rather than public popularity contests.

---

## III. Non-Permanent Governance and Rotating Stewardship

The protocol rejects the concept of a "professional politician," "legislator," or permanent administrative elite.

1. **Task-Specific Assemblies:** When a complex coordination problem arises within the Coordinate Sphere (e.g., redesigning an energy grid node or updating public health safety thresholds), a temporary, randomized or merit-verified **Stewardship Assembly** is convened from the active skill pool.
2. **Strict Term Limits and Immediate Dissolution:** Once the specific optimization or policy adjustment is mathematically validated and deployed, the assembly immediately dissolves. Members return to their personal lives and time sovereignty; no permanent office, institutional salary, or lingering legislative authority is retained.
3. **Open Auditability:** Every decision, vote, and resource allocation made by any temporary assembly is permanently recorded on the Transparent Data Center ledger for public audit, eliminating backroom deals or bureaucratic drift.

## Summary

The Public Bounds module completes the constitutional architecture of the Decentralized Social Protocol. By strictly walling off an **Inviolable Sphere** of absolute individual freedom and limiting collective governance to a transparent, non-permanent, and strictly bounded **Coordinate Sphere**, the protocol proves that complex modern civilization can coordinate its survival and infrastructure without ever sacrificing human dignity, autonomy, or time on the altar of the collective.
